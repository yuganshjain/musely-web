Privacy Policy

Simple, honest, no surprises.

The short version

  • ✓ Local only Your paired computers and settings stay on your device — we never upload them.
  • ✓ No tracking No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no profiling.
  • ✓ Bluetooth only Your phone talks directly to your computer over Bluetooth. Nothing passes through our servers — we don't have any.
  • ✓ No account Musely works without creating an account or providing any personal information.

Bluetooth

Musely connects to your computer as a standard Bluetooth Low Energy mouse and keyboard. This connection is direct, device-to-device — your taps, keystrokes and pointer movements travel straight from your iPhone to the computer you paired with. They are never sent to Musely or to any server, because Musely doesn't run one.

In-app purchases

Musely Pro is a one-time unlock, processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment information — Apple's Privacy Policy governs the transaction.

Third-party services

Musely does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporting services. The only external integration is:

  • Apple StoreKit — for the Musely Pro purchase

Children

Musely does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal data leaves your device, Musely is safe for all ages.

Your rights

Because we don't collect your data on any server, there's nothing to request, export or delete from us. To forget a paired computer or reset your settings, use the app's own Settings screen — everything updates on your device immediately.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date below. We will never introduce data collection without a clear update to this page.
